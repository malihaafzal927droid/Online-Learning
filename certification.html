Course Completion Certificate

At Online Learning, students receive a certificate after successfully completing their selected course.

How to Get Your Certificate?

  1. Choose and enroll in a course.
  2. Complete all course lessons.
  3. Complete the required course activities.
  4. Successfully finish the course.
  5. Receive your course completion certificate.

Available Course Certificates

  • HTML Certificate
  • JavaScript Certificate
  • Python Programming Certificate
  • Web Development Certificate
  • Graphic Designing Certificate
  • Digital Marketing Certificate

Why Get a Certificate?

  • Showcase your skills
  • Add your achievement to your CV
  • Build confidence in your abilities
  • Share your achievement with others
